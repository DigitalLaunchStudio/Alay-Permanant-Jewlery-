Ways to Collaborate

Every campus is different. Each collaboration is tailored to your school's goals, budget, event size and audience. Pricing, discounts and any customized products are confirmed with you after we review your plans, so nothing is fixed in advance.

School-Sponsored Experiences

Great forHonor and scholarship recipients, orientation, homecoming, graduation, and student and staff appreciation.

The school covers all or part of the cost, so students, faculty or staff receive permanent jewelry free or at a reduced rate. This can be a set number of experiences at an agreed-upon rate, or a shared cost, for example 50%, with students paying the balance.

Campus Pop-Ups
