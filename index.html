Frequently Asked Questions
How do private events work?

Send us your event details through the form above. We reply with a custom quote, then set up a full permanent jewelry bar at your venue with a table, chairs, welder, chains and waiver sign-in. All we need from you is a small space and an outlet nearby.

What does it cost to book an event?

A $75 deposit holds your date. The $500 event minimum covers up to 7–8 bracelets, and any additional pieces or add-ons, like charms and birthstones, are added at the party. Guest pricing is $60 for sterling silver and $75 for 14K gold filled. Travel is confirmed per venue.

How much space do you need?

Not much. We can set up on a 4 × 6 ft table, so we work in smaller spaces too. We bring the chains, welder, display and waiver sign-in; all we need is an outlet nearby.

Where do you travel?

We serve South Jersey and Philadelphia, including Haddonfield, Moorestown, Collingswood and Sicklerville. Travel is confirmed per venue when we send your quote.

How far ahead should I book?

We recommend 3–4 weeks ahead so we can curate pieces for your event. Have a tighter timeline? Send an inquiry anyway and we'll do our best.

Can you host at my studio or business?

Yes. We partner with Pilates, barre and yoga studios, salons and boutiques for grand openings, member nights and community events. Pop-ups can be no cost to your space, and we also offer giveaway bundles and prepaid packages. See our partnerships page.

What is permanent jewelry?

A fine chain is fitted to your wrist, ankle, neck, or finger and fused shut with a small welded link, no clasp needed. It's meant to stay on until you're ready to take it off.

Does the welding process hurt?

No. We use a small pulse welder that fuses the chain in a fraction of a second. You may feel a light flash of warmth, but it isn't a burn and there's no needle involved.

Can I shower, swim, and sleep in it?

Yes, that's the point. Our chains are made to be worn every day, in the shower, at the gym, and in the pool or ocean. Just towel-dry it gently after extended water exposure to keep it looking its best.

What if I want to take it off, or it catches on something?

You can snip it off at home with scissors or nail clippers whenever you're ready, or stop by and we'll cut it for you. If it happens to break, bring it back and we'll reweld it.

Is it safe for sensitive skin?

We use genuine sterling silver and 14K gold filled chains, and there is no needle involved. If you have a known metal sensitivity or allergy, tell us before your fitting. Every guest signs a short waiver first.

Will it tarnish or turn my skin green?

We work in genuine sterling silver and 14K gold filled chains, with no plating that flakes or fades. With normal wear, your chain will keep its finish for the long run.

How do I book an individual appointment?

You can book online anytime with our appointment calendar. A $20 deposit is required to reserve your spot. Most single pieces take about 10–15 minutes from choosing your chain to the final weld.
